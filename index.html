Explainer: Career network and agent ideas

Friends you make at work fade away

  1. In one interview, the interviewer stopped the formal talk. He spoke openly about how hard the team was.
  2. He said: let's connect on LinkedIn. After that, he was only a LinkedIn connection.
  3. The same happens when you leave a company. Old colleagues drift away, because staying in touch takes effort.

Choose who can see you, and which circle each friend joins

  1. Each user picks an open profile that anyone can see, or a closed profile for friends only.
  2. Each connection joins your private circle for friends, or your professional circle for work.
  3. When you look for a job, you open your professional circle to more people.

Meet strangers who like the same things

  1. You add your interests to your profile. In this example, the interest is databases.
  2. The app takes all users who like databases. It picks one of them at random.
  3. The app sets up a meetup for you two. Slack's Donut app does this inside one company, but this works across companies.

An agent finds real jobs and applies for you, using paid credits

  1. The job agent is a program that works for you. It scrapes job posts from job sites.
  2. It checks each post against the company's own careers page. A post that does not match is dropped.
  3. The job agent applies to the real posts for you. No humans are involved.
  4. When applications get no answer, outreach mode starts. The job agent messages people in your network for you.
  5. Connections and meetups are free, because nobody pays to make connections.
  6. The job agent runs on paid credits. This is how the app makes money.

A voice tutor teaches any subject

  1. Second idea: a voice tutor. It is an AI that teaches you by talking.
  2. You ask a question out loud. The voice tutor answers out loud.
  3. It works for any subject. You choose what to learn.

An onboarding agent sets up a merchant by voice

  1. Third idea: an onboarding agent for merchants. A merchant is a shop or a business that takes payments.
  2. The onboarding agent talks to the merchant. Its voice comes from ElevenLabs, a voice AI service.
  3. The onboarding agent helps the merchant create a merchant connect PIN.

One event app organizes your whole event

  1. Fourth idea: one event app that organizes an event for you, from start to end.
  2. The event app connects on its own to other apps, such as Snabbit.
  3. It uses those apps to organize the whole event. You do not open each app yourself.

A farmland app looks after land for owners who live far away

  1. Fifth idea: a farmland app for landowners who live far away from their land.
  2. The farmland app leases the land to a farmer for the owner.
  3. It tracks the land and keeps it maintained. The owner gets updates from far away.

One network keeps your work friends, and four more apps do tasks for you

  1. Today, friends you make at work fade away.
  2. Open or closed profiles, two circles and random meetups keep those friends close.
  3. The job agent finds real jobs and applies. Connections are free, and the job agent costs credits.
  4. A voice tutor teaches any subject. An onboarding agent sets up merchants by voice.
  5. One event app organizes your whole event. A farmland app looks after land for owners who live far away.
